# Rally Puddles

`rally_puddles.gd` owns the deterministic catalogue, route placement, prefabricated mask textures and per-contact response. The stage session rebuilds it after collision geometry becomes available, including stage transitions. Stage-ID seeds keep the layout fixed across reloads. Weather changes fill, never position.

## Spatial Distribution & Topography

Puddles avoid artificial "single-file centerline" placement and repetitive silhouettes:

1. **Topographic Wetness Index (TWI)**:
   - Longitudinal slope and vertical route concavity ($\kappa_v = s_{next} - s_{prev}$) are evaluated along the track.
   - Steep slopes ($> 12\%$) and hill crests ($\kappa_v < -0.025$) drain rapidly and reject puddles.
   - Valleys, compressions, and flat zones accumulate water ($twi > 1.0$), boosting `washout_compression_basin`, `sheet_cross_flood`, `ditch_drain_channel`, and `rut_trough_deep`.
2. **Poisson Cluster Process & Anti-Repetition**:
   - Puddles form wet zones / clusters of 1 to 5 puddles (1 primary puddle + 0–4 satellites) separated by dry stretches of 34–74 m.
   - Weighted contextual selection avoids repeating identical archetypes back-to-back and balances all 4 hazard families across the stage.
3. **Family-Aware Lateral Offset & Orientation**:
   - `rut` profiles sit in wheel tracks ($0.65\text{–}1.45\text{ m}$, or $0.25\text{–}0.85\text{ m}$ for `twin_rut_split`) aligned tightly with road heading.
   - `gutter` profiles sit along shoulders/ditches ($1.05\text{–}1.72\text{ m}$ center, so their outer cutbank hugs the $1.9\text{–}2.6\text{ m}$ shoulder/ditch edge), boosted on the inside of curves ($75\%$ probability), with `orient_to_edge` flipping `uv_scale.x` so the steep cutbank always faces the outer edge/ditch.
   - `sheet_cross_flood` spans close to lane center ($0.20\text{–}0.95\text{ m}$) to create unavoidable full-width aquaplaning zones.
   - Compact `pothole` and sprawling `sheet` pools rotate freely ($0\text{..}2\pi$) around the surface normal.

## Adaptive Conformal Mesh ($5 \times 5$ to $7 \times 7$ Grid)
- Rather than a flat rigid quad that gets rejected by cambered road borders, puddles are built as an adaptive subdivided mesh (`5×5` for compact craters, `6×6` for wide sheets/basins, `7×7` for long 6–7 m ruts and drainage channels) using `SurfaceTool`.
- Each vertex is raycast onto `Collision_Road` to hug local ruts, bumps, and shoulder slopes.
- Vertices outside the road boundary have their heights smoothed and receive vertex color `COLOR.r = 0.0`. The shader uses this to smoothly feather the water edge prior to the road boundary, eliminating floating triangles.

## Double-Zone PBR Shading & Visual Depth Optics
`rally_puddle.gdshader` decomposes puddles into two concentric physical zones with distinct depth perception cues:
1. **Outer Wet Soil Ring** (`mask` between 0.006 and 0.26):
   - Simulates soaked mud around the puddle meniscus.
   - Multiplicatively darkens the dirt road underneath (`ALBEDO = vec3(0.012, 0.009, 0.007)`).
   - Satin damp soil roughness ($0.44$), moderate specular ($0.24$), and subtle transparency ($0.40$).
2. **Inner Liquid Basin** (`mask > 0.18`):
   - **Per-Profile Beer-Lambert Turbidity & Shore Absorption**: exponential light extinction through suspended mud ($\text{turbidity} = 1 - \exp(-\text{depth} \cdot \text{turbidity\_rate})$), where `turbidity_rate` ranges from `0.035` (clear shallow glazes) to `0.092` (opaque muddy craters), combined with a `0..5.5 mm` shore absorption gradient (`shore_depth`) so the water edge blends organically into the wet road.
   - **Cavity Rim Occlusion**: deep potholes generate an internal embankment shadow along their perimeter ($1 - \text{rim} \cdot \text{depth\_norm} \cdot 0.58$), giving them a distinct sunken 3D hollow appearance.
   - **Meniscus Surface Tension Glint**: subtle specular highlight where water surface tension contacts the damp soil boundary.
   - **Active Wave & Raindrop Reflection Perturbation**: combines scrolling `water_detail_sample` normal slopes (`wd.yz`) and concentric `rain_drop_ripples(world_position.xz, TIME, rain)` (damped near the shore by surface tension) to distort the sky cubemap lookup vector `reflected`.
   - **Physical Schlick Fresnel & Glossy PBR Response**: low liquid roughness (`0.06`), high specular (`0.86`), and a Schlick Fresnel curve ($F = 0.03 + 0.65 \cdot (1 - \mathbf{N}\cdot\mathbf{V})^{3.6}$) keep puddles translucent and dark when viewed from above while mirroring the sky at grazing angles.

## Dry Weather Protection & Liquid/Snow Basin Contract
- In dry weather (`precipitation_mm_h < 0.05` without antecedent rain), initial surface water is strictly `0.0 mm`.
- Each puddle profile defines a minimum activation threshold (`min_water`). When background liquid water (`water_mm`) is below the threshold, `fill` is `0.0` and the mesh is hidden (`visible = false`). No puddles appear on dry roads.
- **Single liquid feed & depth definition**: `background_water(distance)` reads the single unified liquid supply `water_mm` (including snow/ice melt, never added twice). Reported `depth_mm` includes the base water film occupying the depression (`vehicle_simulation.gd` fuses contacts via `max(base_water_mm, puddle_depth_mm)`).
- **Snow patches vs. water puddles (no frozen ice puddles)**: `RallyPuddles` exposes only liquid water puddles (`winter_kind = 0`) and accumulated snow patches (`winter_kind = 1`, when `snow_mm * retention >= snow_threshold_mm`). Localized frozen puddle ice (`winter_kind = 2`) is disabled; frozen water is excluded from liquid `fill` so hydrodynamic puddle drag never applies to frozen basins.

## Physics: Water Aquaplaning vs. Snow Compaction/Plowing
1. **Water Puddles (`contact_response`)**:
   - **Two-Phase Hydrodynamic Resistance**: continuous viscous/quadratic drag $(150 v + 2750 v^2) \cdot w_{\text{tire}} \cdot h_{\text{water}} \cdot \text{drag\_scale}$ plus entry slam (`22.5 * width * delta_depth_s * v * drag_scale`, capped at $31.25\%$ of normal load); total drag capped at $81.25\%$ of normal load.
   - **Wheel Braking & Steering Pull**: $\tau = F_{\text{drag}} \cdot 0.75 \cdot r_{\text{wheel}}$ and `steering_pull_nm` $= F_{\text{drag}} \cdot 0.07$.
   - **Differentiated Aquaplaning**: longitudinal support drops to $20\%\text{–}45\%$, while lateral cornering support drops severely to $6\%\text{–}25\%$ ($16\text{ m/s}$ dry vs $28\text{ m/s}$ wet evacuation).
2. **Snow Patches (`snow_contact_response`)**:
   - **High Grip Retention (No Aquaplaning Washout)**: longitudinal support stays $\ge 86\%$ and lateral support stays $\ge 78\%$, keeping the car responsive to steering while still triggering snow tracks/VFX.
   - **Strong Compaction & Plowing Braking**: $(9500 \cdot \text{smoothstep}(0, 2, v) + 1800 v + 3600 v^2) \cdot w_{\text{tire}} \cdot h_{\text{snow}} \cdot \text{drag\_scale}$ decelerates the vehicle firmly even at low/medium speeds, paired with a heavier entry impact slam (`34.0 * width * delta_depth_s * v * drag_scale`, capped at $45\%$ load; total drag capped at $95\%$ normal load), stronger wheel braking torque ($0.95 \cdot r_{\text{wheel}}$), and stronger steering tug ($0.11 \cdot F_{\text{drag}}$).
