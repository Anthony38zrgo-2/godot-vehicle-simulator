# FulviaHF — Rally Trophy native engine/exhaust bank

Source: Rally Trophy Data/Cars/car_13.bzf; Sounds.ini is copied for provenance.
Regenerate with `python tools/audio/rally_trophy_fulvia.py`.
Preserve the eight original EE/EX WAV files byte-for-byte. The manifest separates
native recordings, filtered coast loops, derived lift events and synthesized generic
gearbox tones. Do not describe derived sounds as additional authentic recordings.
Consumer: shared rally_vehicle_audio.gd, selected by engine_bank_manifest_path.
Keep this bank independent of Escort samples and calibration.
